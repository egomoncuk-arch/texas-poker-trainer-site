What it is

What is Texas Poker Trainer

Texas Poker Trainer is a mobile app for learning and practicing Texas poker. It is designed as a trainer: you review hands, compare possible actions, and learn to understand game situations more clearly. The app is not a gambling platform and does not support real-money betting.

App features

PRO hints

Hints for deeper learning

PRO hints help you pay attention to important decision factors: position, hand strength, pot size, and potential action value. Hints do not guarantee winnings and are intended only for educational practice.

Example hint

“Compare the risk and potential value of the action before continuing the hand.”

Important

No real money — virtual chips only

Texas Poker Trainer is an educational trainer for users 21+. The app does not offer real-money gambling, does not accept real-money wagers, and does not pay cash prizes. All chips, balances, and game values are virtual and are used only for educational simulation.
